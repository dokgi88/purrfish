Purrfish: Cozy Cat Fishing

Pixel Fishing & Aquarium

Grab your rod and your wetsuit! Fish 120 species, dive to photograph sea life and meet rare cat mermaids across 10 cozy spots.

Grab your rod, little cat — the water is full of surprises!

Purrfish is a cozy pixel-art fishing game you can play with one thumb. Cast your line, guide the lure toward the fish you can see swimming below, then reel them in with a satisfying catch bar.

  • One-thumb fishinghold to cast, drag to steer the lure, hold to reel. Tap the moment the bobber dips for a head start (optional), and enjoy ripples, splashes, gentle vibration and coins flying into your wallet
  • 120 species across 10 spotsMeadow Pond, Mountain River, Sunny Sea, Coral Reef, Frozen Lake, The Abyss, the Sunken Temple, Cloud Lake, Volcano Isle and the Mangrove Forest
  • Hunt rare Golden fish, schools of rare fish and legends like the Moon Carp and the Star Whale, and battle each spot's giant Master once a day
  • Dive & discoverput on a wetsuit, swim each spot to photograph creatures you can't catch for your Photo Book, and find hidden deep caves with your flashlight
  • Cat mermaidsone rare cat mermaid lives in each spot, rarer than any legend. Welcome her to your aquarium or set her free for a lucky blessing
  • 25 lures in 5 tiersbetter lures tempt rarer fish, special lures call mermaids and legends, and a hard-fighting fish can even snap your line
  • A living skyday and night follow your clock, rain, snow and rainbows change what bites, shooting stars give pearls when tapped, and seasons and the real moon phase change the scenery. Each spot shows a 6-hour forecast, and sparkling golden ripples mark where big fish bite
  • Fishdexmany fish have a favorite time (night, rain, dawn or a season), your biggest catches win Gold Crowns, and every first catch remembers its sky: time, weather, season and spot
  • Aquariumyour fish earn coins even while you're away. Decorate with a Sunken Ship, Coral Arch and more, complete Tank Sets for bonus income, share Tank Layouts with a short code, and tap a fish to see where it was caught and how many days you've been together
  • Cat life & collectionhats from a Frog Hat to a Crown, fur colors, seasonal hats and a cozy Cat's Spot. Send a family cat on a 3-hour Cat Trip for pixel postcards, and read its Cat's Diary when you come back
  • Neighbors & festivalsneighbor cats visit your aquarium and leave souvenirs, and Friendship brings new lines, small gifts and a best-pal hat. Weekly Tournament, Monthly Group Catch goals, This Week's Visitor fish, and 5 festivals a year (Cherry Blossom, Summer, Halloween, Christmas, New Year) with stamps to trade for exclusive decor you keep forever
  • Read bottle letters from Captain Whiskers, an old lighthouse-keeper cat, and help restore his lighthouse step by step
  • Grow at your own paceupgrade your Rod, Reel, Line, Bait and Tank, make bait from a catch to target that species (legends excepted), plus Daily Requests, a Daily Gift (miss a day and you keep your progress) and gifts for days together
  • Cozy extrasScenery view hides the buttons so you can just watch the landscape, the Fishing Journal keeps a Photo Album of your last 12 aquarium and Cat's Spot photos, and a little critter hides in the scenery each day for pearls
  • Play offline, anytime

Free to play. Optional in-app purchases: Pearl packs, a Starter Pack and a Supporter Pack (ad rewards without watching ads, a Crown hat and double the offline earnings limit). You can catch all 120 fish without spending a thing.

No forced ads and no banners. Ads are optional rewards you choose to watch, like selling a catch for double coins.

Relax, cast your line, and see what's biting today!
